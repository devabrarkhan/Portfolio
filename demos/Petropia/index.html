Frequently Asked Questions

How strict is the adoption vetting process?

We maintain a rigorous standard to ensure our pets are placed in safe, loving, and capable environments. This involves a background check, home video tour, and a virtual consultation.

Are veterinary services included in adoption?

Your first comprehensive veterinary checkup and a 6-month premium health insurance plan are complementary with every adoption through Petropia.

Can I book services for non-Petropia adopted pets?

Absolutely. Our luxury ecosystem of vets, groomers, and boarders is open to all premium pet owners seeking the highest standard of care.
